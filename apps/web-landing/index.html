How it works

  2. 2

FAQ

Good questions

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Scanning the QR code opens SnapShare in the phone's browser with the camera ready. There's an optional app for guests who'd like one.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on any modern phone. iPhone photos in HEIC format are converted automatically.

What if the venue has bad reception?

Photos are kept safely on the guest's phone and upload as soon as they get signal again, even after the browser is closed and reopened.

Can I stop something embarrassing going on the big screen?

Turn on photo approval and nothing appears until you approve it. You can hide or delete any photo at any time, and hidden photos stop being viewable straight away.

Who can see our photos?

Only people with your event's QR code or link, and you can add a passcode. Location data is removed from every photo.

Do we get the full-quality photos?

Yes. Download every original in one ZIP, or only your favourites, named in order with each photographer's name.
